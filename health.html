Bulking adjustment guide
Weigh daily, use weekly average. If weekly gain is under 0.3kg, add +300 kcal/day. If 0.3–0.6kg, add +150 kcal/day. If 0.6–0.9kg, perfect. If over 1.1kg after week 2, remove 200 kcal/day.
